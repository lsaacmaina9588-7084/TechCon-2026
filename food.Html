My favorite food recipe👍

  1. Put beef in sufuria with salt and add one cup of water.
    Boil for 15 minutes till water dries
  2. Add 3 tablespoon of oil, add sliced onions .Fry till dark brown
  3. Add garlic/gimger,pilau masala,cumin and stir for 1 minute
  4. Add chopped tomatoes,cook till paste
  5. Wash rice,add to sufuria .Mix well so rice gets coated
  6. Add 3 cups of water,add salt then test after water boils
  7. cover and allow it to heat under low heat for about 20-25 minutes.
    when the water is gone, turn off and leave covered 10 minutes
